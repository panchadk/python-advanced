groupby

df.groupby('k')['v'].sum()

df.groupby('k').agg( tot=('v','sum'), avg=('v','mean'))
multi-aggregation
df.groupby('k').size()
rows per group

Pivot

df.pivot_table( values='v', index='r', columns='c', aggfunc='sum')
grid layout

Merge / join

a.merge(b, on='key')
join on column
how='left'
keep all left rows
how='inner'
matches only
how='outer'
keep everything

Reshape

df.melt(id_vars='id')

Dates

pd.to_datetime(df['d'])

s.dt.month s.dt.weekday
extract parts
df.groupby(df.d.dt.month)

df.groupby(key)[col].agg(...) answers almost every 'what's the X per Y?' question.
