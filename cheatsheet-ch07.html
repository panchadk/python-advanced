Hypothesis tests

stats.ttest_ind(a, b)
two independent groups
stats.ttest_rel(a, b)
paired (e.g. CV folds)
p < 0.05

Confidence interval

stats.sem(data)
standard error
stats.t.interval(0.95, n-1, loc=m, scale=sem)
95% CI
Read p-values carefully

A p-value is NOT the probability your hypothesis is true. 'Not significant' means 'not enough evidence', not 'no effect'.
