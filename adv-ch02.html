Lesson

Selecting columns and rows

Grab a column with df["col"]. Pick rows by label with .loc and by position with .iloc. This label-vs-position distinction is the single most important thing to master in pandas.

In [ ]: python3
TIP .loc is label-based and inclusive of the end (0:2 gives rows 0,1,2). .iloc is position-based and exclusive like normal Python slicing. Mixing them up is the #1 pandas beginner bug.

Missing data

Real data has holes. pandas marks them NaN. You detect them with isna(), drop them with dropna(), or fill them with fillna().

In [ ]: python3
WHY IT MATTERS Deciding how to handle missing data — drop rows, fill with a mean, or leave as NaN for the model — is a real modeling choice, not just cleanup. We go deeper in the data-cleaning chapter.
