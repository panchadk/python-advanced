Confusion matrix

from sklearn.metrics import confusion_matrix

Metrics

precision_score(y,p)
of predicted +, how many right
recall_score(y,p)
of actual +, how many caught
f1_score(y,p)
balance of the two
roc_auc_score(y, proba)
ranking quality (0.5=random)
Accuracy isn't enough

On imbalanced data a model can be 99% accurate and useless. Different errors (false neg vs false pos) carry different costs.
